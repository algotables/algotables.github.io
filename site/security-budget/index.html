Security Budget

In the last 30 days validators were paid 6.33M ALGO, and fees covered 0.50% of it.

Every block pays its proposer from the FeeSink: half the block's fees plus a bonus the Foundation funds. The bonus started at 10 ALGO a block in January 2025 and drops 1% every million blocks, and the Foundation has committed to it through January 2028. This page tracks what the bonus costs, how much fees cover, and how long the FeeSink lasts.
